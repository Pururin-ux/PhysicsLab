# 0007 — Optional bridge from mechanical to internal energy

Status: `PROVISIONAL`. Scope: the authored Grade VII and VIII textbook chapters.

## Problem

The Grade VII explanation says that mechanical energy is not conserved when
friction or air resistance matters, but leaves the destination of that energy
unexplained. The Grade VIII chapter introduces internal energy without using
this earlier question. The automatic next-chapter link intentionally stops at
the grade boundary.

## Decision

- Ask where part of the mechanical energy goes at the end of the Grade VII
  explanation. Add a direct, optional link to the Grade VIII chapter from that
  chapter and its topic entry.
- Begin the Grade VIII chapter with the same resisted-motion situation and
  explain that part of the mechanical energy becomes internal energy of the
  body and surrounding air. The existing ideal, resistance-free worked example
  and practice remain unchanged.
- Keep the grade boundary in automatic chapter navigation. This connection is
  an authored question, not a required curriculum path or completion claim.

The [Belarusian VII–IX programme](../research/sources/belarus-2026/Fizika-7-9.txt)
places conservation of mechanical energy in Grade VII and internal energy and
its changes at the start of Grade VIII; the relevant textbook sections are
recorded in the [source register](../research/belarus-source-register-2026-09-08.md).
The exact cross-grade link is a product hypothesis, not a prescribed step.
