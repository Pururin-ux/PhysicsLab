# Production Sprint - 2026-06-08

Goal: start a useful, visually recognizable physics/math Telegram channel
without locking into one template or AI-looking style.

Sprint length: 14 days.

## Success Signals

Track manually at first:

- saves;
- forwards;
- comments/replies;
- poll answers;
- profile/channel joins after post;
- which topic gets reused by students in questions.

Do not optimize for likes alone. Saveable physics content should win through
forwards and saves.

## Content Pillars

1. **Saveable physics traps**
   - exact signs, units, axes, formulas;
   - best for Telegram portrait.

2. **One-task breakdowns**
   - one problem, one diagram, one answer;
   - best for carousel or portrait.

3. **Math survival cards**
   - formulas, graphs, shortcuts that are allowed and correct;
   - best for portrait or carousel.

4. **Memes with a rule**
   - one joke plus one concept;
   - best for square Telegram or TikTok opener.

5. **Study rhythm**
   - realistic summer/exam routine;
   - best for softer style lanes and polls.

## First Work Package

Post slug:

`minus-acceleration-not-braking`

Educational point:

Минус у ускорения показывает направление проекции относительно выбранной оси.
Он не всегда означает торможение. Торможение происходит, когда скорость и
ускорение направлены в разные стороны.

Audience:

- 9-11 класс;
- physics basics / kinematics;
- students who confuse signs in formulas and graphs.

Copy candidates:

1. Plain:
   - Title: `Минус в ускорении`
   - Subtitle: `это про ось, а не сразу про торможение`
   - Key line: `если v и a в одну сторону - скорость растёт`
   - Key line: `если в разные - скорость падает`

2. Slightly meme-aware:
   - Title: `Минус не виноват`
   - Subtitle: `сначала подпиши ось`
   - Key line: `ось вправо -> вправо плюс`
   - Key line: `a < 0 значит "влево", а не "всё плохо"`

3. Calmer:
   - Title: `Как читать знак ускорения`
   - Subtitle: `одна ось решает половину путаницы`
   - Key line: `знак - это направление проекции`
   - Key line: `сравни направление скорости и ускорения`

Recommended direction:

Use candidate 2 for hook, but keep body wording plain. It is memorable without
forced slang.

## First Image Requirements

Telegram portrait:

- `1080x1350`;
- large readable title;
- two mini-cases with arrows:
  - `v` and `a` same direction -> speed grows;
  - `v` and `a` opposite directions -> speed falls;
- one small axis diagram;
- one recurring character or sticker on the side, not under text;
- no generated final text;
- deterministic formulas and labels.

TikTok sequence later:

1. Hook: `Минус не виноват`
2. Misconception: `a < 0 = тормозит? не всегда`
3. Axis: `ось вправо -> вправо плюс`
4. Compare arrows: `v` vs `a`
5. Check yourself: one quick example.
