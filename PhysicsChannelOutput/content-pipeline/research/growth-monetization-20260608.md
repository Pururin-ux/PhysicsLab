## Practical Conclusions

### 1. Telegram growth needs searchable utility

The channel should not be only "pretty cards." Public Telegram discovery and
post search make exact topic naming useful. Each saveable post should include
clear keywords in the caption:

- "ускорение";
- "ось координат";
- "кинематика";
- "ЕГЭ физика";
- "ОГЭ математика";
- "формулы";
- "разбор задачи".

Image text can stay playful, but caption text should be searchable and precise.

### 2. Monetization has stages

Near term:

- build reach, saves, forwards, comments, and recognizable format;
- prepare posts that sponsors can understand: audience, topic, grade, utility;
- avoid looking like an answer-leak or spam channel.

Medium term:

- Telegram ad revenue sharing can matter after the channel reaches platform
  thresholds and an eligible setup;
- direct ad placements and suggested posts become more realistic once the
  channel has a visible niche and stable engagement;
- PDFs, mini-guides, and paid problem sets can become a cleaner education
  product than random ads.

Long term:

- sell sponsorship slots only when the channel has a content calendar and
  quality bar;
- keep ads clearly separated from educational trust;
- track which formats lead to saves/forwards before optimizing for money.

### 3. Best content mix

Use a 5-pillar rhythm:

1. Saveable cheat sheets: formulas, signs, units, common traps.
2. Micro-guides: one misconception, one fix, one example.
3. Meme hooks: short, specific school pain, not forced slang.
4. Task breakdowns: given -> diagram -> formula -> answer.
5. Calm study planning: realistic repetition plans without moralizing.

Recommended weekly mix:

- 2 saveable cards;
- 1 task breakdown;
- 1 meme / hook;
- 1 short carousel or TikTok-card sequence;
- 1 poll / question / "choose the answer" post.

### 4. Copy should be useful, not "teen cosplay"

The public Telegram scan confirms that direct utility beats fake youth slang.
Good copy sounds like a smart classmate:

- short;
- concrete;
- exact school context;
- one light joke max;
- no motivational foam.

Reject:

- "ты легенда";
- "забирай скорее";
- "прокачай себя";
- hype emojis;
- adult-coded attempts at slang;
- generic "you got this" language on every post.

### 5. TikTok/card reposting needs different pacing

Telegram portrait posts can be dense. TikTok cards need more sequential
drama:

1. hook frame;
2. misconception frame;
3. visual explanation;
4. mini-example;
5. "save/check yourself" payoff.

Default exports:

- Telegram portrait: `1080x1350`;
- Telegram square meme: `1080x1080`;
- TikTok/story sequence: `1080x1920`;
- carousel cards: same size across a sequence.

### 6. Learning science rules for this channel

Use:

- retrieval prompts: "проверь себя", "что будет со знаком?";
- worked examples: one fully solved micro-task;
- signaling: underline the exact sign/unit/axis that matters;
- spacing: repeat the same concept in a new format later, not on the same day;
- common error framing: "где теряют баллы".

Avoid:

- too many facts per card;
- tiny derivations;
- decorative diagrams that do not teach;
- jokes that hide the rule;
- generated formulas or fake labels.

### 7. Visual generation rule

Use ImageGen / ChatGPT Image for original visual drafts and character/sticker
layers. Do not accept generated readable Russian text or formulas as final.

For recurring characters:

- create an approved character sheet first;
- reuse cutouts where possible;
- if regenerating, lock identity and change only pose/emotion/context;
- reject identity drift.

## Immediate Production Plan

Start with a two-week sprint:

- create a stable character draft;
- publish useful posts before pure memes;
- make every caption searchable;
- test 4 visual lanes, but keep one voice;
- track which posts are most saveable.
