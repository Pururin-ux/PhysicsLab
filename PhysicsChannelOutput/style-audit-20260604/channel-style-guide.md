## Core Vibe

Friendly internet notebook, not polished edtech.

The channel should feel like a smart classmate making physics/math survivable:
- meme-literate;
- slightly chaotic;
- emotionally honest;
- readable and useful;
- original, not copied from references;
- visibly human, not corporate or glossy AI.

## Visual Territories

Use these as reusable directions.

### 1. Koto-Meme Explainer

Small absurd animal, blunt caption, simple educational punchline.

Use for:
- formula traps;
- common mistakes;
- "when you forgot units" jokes;
- quick reminders.

Traits:
- square format;
- bold high-contrast text;
- compressed/cropped image feel;
- one joke plus one concept.

### 2. Notebook Sketch

Light paper background, rough black line, doodles, arrows, handwritten side notes.

Use for:
- formulas;
- task breakdowns;
- diagrams;
- "на пальцах" explanations.

Traits:
- off-white or pale gray;
- rough strokes;
- minimal color;
- formulas rendered exactly with deterministic tools.

### 3. Melancholy Study Night

Dark, muted, late-night, blue/green/black mood.

Use sparingly for:
- burnout jokes;
- exam anxiety;
- "решаю физику в 2:17" posts;
- serious reflective posts.

Traits:
- low brightness;
- cropped photos/illustrations;
- small text blocks;
- no glossy neon sci-fi.

### 4. Anime/Internet Collage

Anime crop, screenshot fragment, sticker, meme text, classroom/internet mashup.

Use for:
- carousel openers;
- relatable school scenes;
- "teacher vs student logic" posts.

Traits:
- mixed media;
- imperfect crops;
- labels and arrows;
- one dominant visual, not a full chaotic wall.

### 5. Loud Cringe-Retro Accent

High saturation orange/green/pink, old-web compression, intentionally unserious.

Use only as accent:
- "важно!";
- final answer reveal;
- meme climax;
- warning sticker.

Traits:
- saturated sticker blocks;
- outlined text;
- small area, high impact.

## Palette

Use a muted base with occasional loud accents:

- Paper: `#F4F1EA`
- Soft paper gray: `#E3DFD8`
- Charcoal: `#24211D`
- Dust brown: `#6E625A`
- Muted clay: `#9E6B55`
- Fog green: `#6E7A61`
- Washed blue-gray: `#7E908E`
- Sticker yellow: `#E8C14A`
- Warning coral: `#E65A3A`
- Meme green: `#55B84A`

Avoid one-note purple/blue gradients and polished SaaS palettes.

## Typography

Use deterministic text rendering.

Recommended roles:
- Main readable sans: Rubik, Nunito, or Comfortaa.
- Handwritten accent: Caveat.
- Formula/code/numbers: JetBrains Mono or KaTeX output.
- Meme caption: heavy bold sans with light stroke or shadow, but not for long explanations.

Rules:
- exact Russian text only;
- no generated text inside ImageGen outputs;
- keep long explanations in deterministic layout;
- use handwritten accents for labels, not paragraphs.

## Composition Rules

- One main idea per image.
- Dense is allowed; clutter without hierarchy is not.
- Use arrows, circles, underlines, side notes, tape, sticky notes, doodle margins.
- Keep formulas large enough for phone reading.
- Prefer portrait `1080x1350` for learning posts.
- Prefer square `1080x1080` for memes.
- Leave safe margins: at least 64 px outer padding for text-heavy cards.
- Make educational content legible before adding jokes.

## Physics/Math Post Grammar

Useful repeated blocks:
- "ловушка"
- "запомни"
- "на пальцах"
- "дано -> найти -> решение"
- "не делай так"
- "если видишь ..., значит ..."
- "ответ: ..."

Visual ingredients:
- rough axes and arrows;
- formula sticker;
- small reaction creature;
- crossed-out wrong path;
- highlighted unit/sign/vector;
- one absurd caption that makes the rule memorable.

## Avoid List

Avoid:
- direct reuse of Pinterest images in final monetized posts;
- glossy photorealistic AI students;
- fake textbooks or fake screenshots;
- generated formulas/text;
- sterile corporate explainers;
- excessive gradients;
- tiny unreadable derivations;
- too many jokes on one card;
- polished "AI poster" lighting;
- mascot work unless explicitly requested.

## Production Method

For publishable images:

1. Write and verify the educational content.
2. Render text/formulas/diagrams deterministically.
3. Add original illustration, texture, or meme layer.
4. Composite final PNG.
5. Inspect final image at phone size.
6. Report paths and any remaining physics/design risk.

Use ImageGen for mood and original visual material, not for exact educational content.
